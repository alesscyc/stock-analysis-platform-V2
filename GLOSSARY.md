# Chart Analysis

Language for automatic technical overlays drawn on the price chart.

## Language

**Swing Zone**:
A completed price range that opens at a pivot high (a bar whose high exceeds the highs of the three bars on each side) and closes at the first later bar whose high reaches at least 99% of that pivot high. Its top is the pivot high; its bottom is the lowest low strictly between the opening and closing bars, or the pivot high when no interior bars exist.
_Avoid_: support/resistance band, swing box
